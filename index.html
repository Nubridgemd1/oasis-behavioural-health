About the Practice

You deserve to be heard, supported, and understood.

At Oasis Behavioural Health, care begins with listening. Lynda Chinyere Agbai is a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) who provides evidence-based, personalized treatment in a warm and nonjudgmental environment. Whether you are navigating anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma, or a mood disorder, you will find a steady partner in your care.

Every treatment plan is tailored to you — thoughtful medication management paired with genuine support, delivered entirely online so care fits your life.
